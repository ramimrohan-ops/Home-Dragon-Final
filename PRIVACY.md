# Home Dragon privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Home Dragon is a pet dragon that lives on your Android home screen. This page explains what the app does with your information.

**Short version: Home Dragon does not collect, store off your phone, share or sell any personal data. It has no internet access.**

## What the app uses on your phone

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Accessibility service ("Home Dragon icon finder"), only after you agree in the app | Finds the position and size of your home-screen icons, reads the launcher's page dots to get the page number (only the two numbers in a label like "Page 2 of 5"), and notices when the home screen is in front, so the dragon can sit on icons and breathe fire at them | Used live in memory only (the icon layout of each page is kept in memory while the icon finder runs; the size of a normal icon is saved on the phone as a number). Not sent anywhere except as below |
| Message to the Phone Status app (my other app, only if it is installed on the same phone) | Tells it "run" or "don't run" (run = the home screen is in front on the page where its widget is, page 2), so its widget only updates while you can see it | One message to that app's package on your phone. It carries "run or not" and a short reason (which can name the page, such as "page 3 of 4"). No screen contents. It leaves the phone nowhere |
| Display over other apps | Draws the dragon on top of your home screen | Nothing is collected |
| Usage access (optional, switched on by you in Android settings) | Tells the dragon which app is in front, so it hides when you open another app. Only the name of the app in front is used | Used live in memory only. Not saved, not sent anywhere |
| Notification | Shows the required "Dragon is on your home screen" notice while the dragon runs | Nothing is collected |
| Start after reboot, background running | Brings the dragon back after a restart and keeps it running | Nothing is collected |

The icon finder checks screen positions of icons, whether an icon has a label (it does not read the label), and the name of the app that is in front. It does not read or store text, messages, passwords or anything you type. It never taps, types or controls anything.

## What is saved on your phone

Your settings (dragon size, speed, quality, particles, transparency, flame colours) and a short crash note are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## What the app does not do

- No internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere
- No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files

## Children

Home Dragon is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Your choices

You can switch the icon finder off at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility, switch usage access off in Android Settings > Apps > Special access > Usage access, and remove the dragon by tapping Stop in the app or uninstalling it.
